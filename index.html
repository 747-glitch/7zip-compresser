How the Progress Bar Breakdown Works

  • 0% – 5%: Initializes the engine and fetches the 7zz.wasm binary.
  • 5% – 20%: Recreates nested folders and reads files into virtual WebAssembly memory.
  • 20% – 98%: Passes the -bsp1 flag to 7-Zip so it tracks completion progress and updates the <progress> bar.
  • 100%: Triggers the local browser download of your .7z file and frees memory.
